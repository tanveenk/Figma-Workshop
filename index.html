SFU Burnaby Campus Guide

Location: Applied Sciences Building (ASB 9705), Simon Fraser University, 8888 University Dr, Burnaby, BC.

🚌 Public Transit

Take the Expo or Millennium SkyTrain to Production Way–University Station, then transfer to the #145 SFU bus directly to the Burnaby Transportation Centre loop.

🚗 Parking & Entrance

Visitor parking is available at West Mall Centre Parkade. Follow the CSSS orange directional signage straight to the Level 9 Atrium check-in tables.

📶 Wi-Fi & Facilities

Connect to SFU-Guest (no password required) or eduroam. Accessible elevators and gender-neutral washrooms located in ASB Level 9.

Code of Conduct

Fall Hacks is dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion.

Our Standards:

  • Use welcoming and inclusive language.
  • Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
  • Focus on what is best for the hacker community.
  • Show empathy towards other community members.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please alert any organizer with a CSSS badge or email conduct@fallhacks.ca.
